# Germany Job Portals, Startup Boards & Tech Networking Communities

A curated link list for job hunting and networking in the German (and especially Hamburg/North Germany) tech scene.

---

##  Hamburg Startup Jobs

| Platform | Link | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Startup City Hamburg  Jobs & Co-Founder | https://startupcity.hamburg/jobs-cofounder | Official Hamburg Invest platform; free job board + co-founder matching |
| Hamburg Startups Job Board | https://hamburg-startups.net | Community job board run by the Hamburg Startups blog/media outlet |
| AI Beavers Hamburg (Build Fridays) | https://www.meetup.com/de-DE/ai-beavers/ | Not a job board, but a weekly in-person spot to meet Hamburg founders/builders hiring informally |

*Hamburg's startup scene skews gaming, e-commerce, logistics, and media (companies like Jimdo, InnoGames, XING got their start here)  worth keywording your search with these industries.*

##  Networking & Community Platforms

### PyData
- Global: https://pydata.org
- PyData Berlin: https://berlin.pydata.org

### PyLadies
- Global: https://www.pyladies.com
- PyLadies Hamburg: https://hamburg.pyladies.com
- Find your city chapter: https://www.pyladies.com/locations/

### Women in AI
- Global: https://www.womeninai.co
- Active as a community partner across German PyCon/PyData events

### AI Beavers
- Meetup (main/Hamburg): https://www.meetup.com/de-DE/ai-beavers/
- Frankfurt chapter: https://www.meetup.com/ai-beavers-frankfurt/
- Events via Luma: https://luma.com/ai_beavers
- Description: informal international builder/founder community, weekly "Build Fridays" in Hamburg and Frankfurt — bring a laptop, work alongside other founders, no pitching/agenda

## Notes
- Local city-based Meetup/Luma searches (e.g. "Hamburg AI meetup") often surface smaller, high-signal communities not listed on major boards.
